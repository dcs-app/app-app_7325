Privacy Policy

How Haveli Restaurant handles your personal information.

This Privacy Policy describes how Haveli Restaurant collects, uses, and protects information you provide when visiting our website or dining with us.

Information We Collect

When you make a reservation, sign up for our newsletter, or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and any preferences you share. Our website uses cookies for analytics and to improve your browsing experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to confirm reservations, send you updates you have opted into, respond to inquiries, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies to remember your preferences and analyze site traffic. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings or via the cookie banner on our site.

Contact

For questions about this policy or to request access, correction, or deletion of your data, please contact us via our contact page.

Updates to This Document

Haveli Restaurant reserves the right to update this document at any time. Material changes will be communicated via the website. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

Questions or Concerns

If you have any questions about this document or wish to exercise your rights, the fastest way to reach us is via the contact form on this site. We aim to respond within two business days. For in-person inquiries, please refer to the addresses and hours listed on our contact page.

Governing Provisions

This document is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which Haveli Restaurant operates. Any disputes will be resolved in the appropriate local courts. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full effect.
